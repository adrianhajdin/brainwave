#### Unmet Medical Needs

**Retinitis Pigmentosa**

Retinitis pigmentosa encompasses a group of inherited retinal degenerative diseases affecting approximately 1 in 4,000 individuals worldwide. Characterized by progressive photoreceptor cell death, the condition typically presents with night blindness and peripheral vision loss in early adulthood, advancing to severe visual impairment or blindness.

Current treatment options are limited to vitamin A supplementation, which provides modest benefit in select genetic subtypes, and gene therapy for patients with RPE65 mutations (representing less than 1% of cases). The majority of patients lack disease-modifying treatment options.

The global prevalence of retinitis pigmentosa is estimated at approximately 1.5 million patients, with significant unmet need for therapies that preserve retinal structure and function across diverse genetic causes.

**Dry Age-Related Macular Degeneration**

Age-related macular degeneration is the leading cause of vision loss in adults over 50 in developed countries. The dry (non-neovascular) form accounts for approximately 80-90% of cases and is characterized by progressive degeneration of photoreceptors and retinal pigment epithelium in the macula, the region responsible for central vision.

Geographic atrophy, the advanced stage of dry AMD, affects approximately 5 million patients globally and currently has limited treatment options. The recent approval of complement inhibitors provides the first therapeutic options but addresses only one aspect of disease pathology and demonstrates modest efficacy in slowing atrophy progression.

Neuroprotective approaches targeting photoreceptor and RPE survival represent a complementary strategy with potential to preserve visual function across disease stages.

**Optic Atrophy**

Optic atrophy, characterized by degeneration of retinal ganglion cells and their axons, occurs in multiple inherited and acquired conditions including glaucoma, traumatic optic neuropathy, and hereditary optic neuropathies.

Glaucoma alone affects over 70 million individuals worldwide, with optic nerve degeneration as the final common pathway of vision loss. Despite intraocular pressure reduction as the mainstay of therapy, many patients continue to experience progressive vision loss.

Neuroprotective therapies targeting retinal ganglion cell survival represent a significant unmet need across the spectrum of optic neuropathies.

- **Professional but accessible:** Write for educated scientific and business audiences without unnecessary jargon
- **Evidence-based:** Support claims with data; avoid hyperbole
- **Patient-focused:** Emphasize patient benefit and unmet medical needs
- **Technically credible:** Demonstrate scientific rigor appropriate for researcher and investor audiences
- **No promotional excess:** Avoid marketing clichés and overstated claims

### Western Audience Considerations
- **Remove all Chinese language elements:** All content in English only
- **Avoid overly emotional language:** Western biotech investors prefer factual, data-driven communication
- **Eliminate grandiose claims:** Replace with specific, measurable statements
- **Professional photography:** Use high-quality scientific imagery, not stock photos of mountains
- **Conservative design:** Clean, modern, professional aesthetic consistent with leading biotech companies

### Content Priority
1. **Science first:** Lead with mechanism, data, and clinical rationale
2. **Clear value proposition:** What makes this approach differentiated?
3. **Credibility markers:** Team credentials, institutional partnerships, peer-reviewed publications
4. **Regulatory pathway:** Clear understanding of development timeline and regulatory strategy
5. **Investment thesis:** Articulate market opportunity, competitive advantages, and financing strategy

### Regulatory Compliance
- **No unapproved product claims:** All products are investigational
- **Forward-looking statement disclaimers:** Required on investor content
- **Privacy compliance:** GDPR-appropriate privacy policy
- **Accurate representation:** All scientific and business statements must be supportable
